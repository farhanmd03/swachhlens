# SwachhLens Brand Asset Catalog & Production Inventory

## Overview
This catalog documents the complete, production-ready brand asset suite for **SwachhLens**, reviewed, curated, and extracted strictly from approved source materials (Canva identity suite and Gemini 8-panel visual-language board). No AI image generation or redesign was performed. All assets have undergone visual inspection, mathematical unmixing to remove baked checkerboards, and multi-background verification.
